# GR Mod 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-11-13

适用于 GR Mod 的 iOS、Android、Windows 和 macOS 版本。
This applies to GR Mod for iOS, Android, Windows and macOS.

## 中文

**GR Mod 不收集任何个人数据。** 没有账号，没有广告，没有统计或跟踪。开发者收不到你的任何数据。

- **文件和照片**：你选择的固件、预设（.xmp / .cube）和图片只在你的设备上处理，保存在 GR Mod 自己的存储空间或你选择的位置，不会上传。
- **Bluetooth® 和无线局域网**：只用来连接你的理光照相机，把固件推送给它。从照相机读到的信息（机型、固件版本、电池电量等）只在设备上使用，记录在本地的推送日志里。只有你轻触[发送日志]时，日志才会通过你选择的方式发送出去。
- **网络请求**：GR Mod 只连接以下地址：
  - 理光官方网站，用于查询和下载官方固件；
  - GitHub 和 jsDelivr，仅电脑版使用，用于检查更新和读取致谢名单。

  这些网站可能按各自的隐私政策记录访问信息（例如 IP 地址）。
- **第三方**：GR Mod 不与任何第三方共享数据，也不含第三方统计或广告组件。

如有问题，请在 GitHub 提交 issue：https://github.com/Maxwooow/Ricoh-GRMod/issues

## English

**GR Mod collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. Nothing about you reaches the developer.

- **Files and photos**: the firmware, presets (.xmp / .cube) and pictures you choose are processed on your device only. They stay in GR Mod's own storage or where you save them, and are never uploaded.
- **Bluetooth and Wi-Fi**: used only to connect to your Ricoh camera and send it the firmware. What is read from the camera (model, firmware version, battery level and so on) is used on the device and kept in a local push log. The log leaves the device only if you tap Send log and choose where it goes.
- **Network requests**: GR Mod connects only to:
  - Ricoh's official website, to look up and download the official firmware;
  - GitHub (and jsDelivr), in the desktop app only, to check for updates and read the acknowledgements.

  Those sites may log requests (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies.
- **Third parties**: GR Mod shares no data with anyone and contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub at https://github.com/Maxwooow/Ricoh-GRMod/issues
